**Title**
Why Do Japanese People Count “Nii, Shii, Roo, Yaa, Too”? 🍊

**Description**
It sounds like a chant—but it’s a way of counting by twos in Japanese\! 🇯🇵

The rhythm “nii, shii, roo, yaa, too” means:

🍊 Nii → 2
🍊 Shii → 4
🍊 Roo → 6
🍊 Yaa → 8
🍊 Too → 10

These shortened number sounds make it quick and rhythmic to count items in pairs. A little everyday Japanese you might not learn in a textbook\! ✨

Try it next time you’re sorting things—and tell us how you count by twos in your language\! 👇
